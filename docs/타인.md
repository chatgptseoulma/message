## 다이어트식단표.html — "식단 설계소" (736 lines)

### Overview
The user enters body data, activity level, and a goal. The page calculates daily calories and macros, then builds a meal plan from real Korean menu items that fits a per-meal budget. Everything re-renders live.
- **Fonts:** Gowun Batang, IBM Plex Mono, and IBM Plex Sans KR (Google Fonts).
- **Images:** `PHOTOS={}` and `CREDITS=[]` are empty (345-346), so every dish shows a placeholder with its first letter, and the credits section is hidden.
- **localStorage `diet-planner-v1`:** `{sex, age, height, weight, job, str, strMin, strDays, cardio, cardioMin, cardioDays, goal, meals, tier}`.
  - It is saved on every render. `picks` (the user's manual menu swaps) is not saved.

### Inputs (form `#form`, 236-317)
- Sex, age, height, and weight, with a live BMI using the Korean cutoffs 18.5 / 23 / 25.
- Job activity: sit, light, active, or heavy.
- Strength training: none, light, mod, or hard, with minutes and days per week.
- Cardio: none, walk, bike, jog, run, or hiit, with minutes and days per week.
- Goal: cut_lean, cut_health, maintain, lean_bulk, or bulk.
- Meals per day: 2, 3, or 4.
- Budget tabs (`#tabs`):
  - 0 = up to 5,000 won
  - 1 = 5,000–10,000 won (the default)
  - 2 = 10,000–15,000 won
  - 3 = 15,000–25,000 won
  - The left and right arrow keys switch tabs.

### Constants (349-452)
- **Activity multipliers:** `JOB` sit 1.2, light 1.35, active 1.5, heavy 1.65.
- **Strength MET values:** `STR` light 3.5, mod 5, hard 6.
- **Cardio MET values:** `CARDIO` walk 4.3, bike 7, jog 8.3, run 10, hiit 8.
- **Goals (`GOALS`):**

  | Goal | Calorie adjustment | Protein (g/kg) | Fat share |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | cut_lean | −20% | 2.2 | 0.25 |
  | cut_health | −15% | 1.5 | 0.30 |
  | maintain | 0 | 1.6 | 0.28 |
  | lean_bulk | +10% | 2.0 | 0.25 |
  | bulk | +18% | 1.8 | 0.25 |

- **Calorie split per meal (`SLOTS`):**
  - 2 meals: 45 / 55
  - 3 meals: 27 / 39 / 34
  - 4 meals: 24 / 33 / snack 13 / 30
- **Food data:**
  - `DISHES`: 39 items. Fields: `{id, t: tier, kinds, name, k, p, c, f, price, where, base?, reduce?, tip}`.
  - `SNACKS`: 4 items.
  - `ADDONS`: 8 items.
  - `REDUCE`: rice −100, noodles −120, bread −70.

### Formulas (`targets(u)`, 482-502)
- BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor) = 10·kg + 6.25·cm − 5·age + (male +5 / female −161)
- Non-exercise expenditure: neat = BMR × JOB
- Exercise calories = (MET−1) × kg × (minutes/60) × days per week/7, calculated separately for strength and cardio
- TDEE = neat + strength + cardio
- kcal = TDEE × (1 + adjustment)
  - When cutting, kcal never goes below max(BMR, 1500 for male / 1200 for female).
  - Rounded to the nearest 10.
- Reference weight refW = the weight at BMI 25 if BMI > 25, otherwise actual weight
- Protein = refW × g/kg, capped at 35% of kcal
- Fat = max(kcal × fat share, 0.6 g/kg × refW × 9) / 9
- Carbs = (kcal − 4P − 9F) / 4
- Weekly weight change = (kcal − TDEE) × 7 / 7700
- Water = 0.033 L/kg

### Meal matching
- **`fit()` (505-528):** a greedy search of up to 5 steps over add-ons and portion reductions.
  - The error combines calorie error (over-target counts ×1.5), protein shortfall or excess, and fat error.
  - Carbs are not part of the error.
- **`goalPenalty`:** cuts penalize meals over 33% fat; maintain penalizes meals over 38% fat.
- **`candidates()`:** when a budget tier has too few breakfast options, it borrows dishes from cheaper tiers (`lower` tag).
- **`plan()` (546-561):**
  - Ranks dishes per meal slot and avoids repeating a dish across slots.
  - Shows 3 alternatives.
  - A user's manual choice is stored in `picks["tier|meals|slot"]`.
- **Rendering:** `render()` (626-685) and `mealCard` (583).
  - The fit pill shows within 8% as a match, within 15% as close, and anything beyond as off.

### Gotchas
- The calorie-floor alert always says "raised to BMR", even when the 1500/1200 floor was the one applied.
- A plan can include both "add half a bowl of rice" and "remove a third of a bowl of rice".
- A manual choice can duplicate a dish used in another slot.
- Strength and cardio are both labeled step "4".
- Snack prices ignore the budget tier.

## 부동산갈아타기.html — "우리집 NEXT" (497 lines)

### Overview
A planner for selling your current apartment and moving to a better one ("갈아타기"). It has four parts: a complex price-gap monitor, complex details, a funding calculator, and a 3D virtual site visit ("임장").
- **All data is synthetic, generated from a seed.** There are no real APIs.
- Units are 억원 (100 million won).
- **External libraries:** Google Fonts (Jua, Gowun Dodum) and **three.js r128 from cdnjs** (242), used only by the 3D view.
- **localStorage `home_next_plan_v1`:** the entire funding object `P`. `buyPrice`, the state `S`, and the filters are not saved.

### Data generation (245-266)
- **Regions:** 6 regions with 27 complexes: Gangnam 5, Songpa 5, Gangdong 4, Gwacheon 4, Bundang 5, Mapo/Yongsan 4.
- **Random seed:**
  - The generator is an LCG.
  - The initial seed is `build(2033)`; refreshing reseeds with `Date.now()%100000`.
- **Complex attributes:**
  - Base price = regional base × (0.75–1.25) × 0.55. Regional bases are Gangnam 24, Songpa 19, Gangdong 13, Gwacheon 16, Bundang 14, Mapo/Yongsan 17.
  - Unit-size multipliers: 59 → 1, 74 → 1.25, 84 → 1.5.
- **Trades:**
  - Spread over the last 360 days.
  - Newer trades get up to +4% drift.
  - 90% are brokered sales (중개거래).
- **Data shape:** `DATA[i] = {id, name, reg, hh, year, trades:{59:[],74:[],84:[]}}`, where each trade is `{d, p, fl, type}`.
- **Statistics (`stats`, 271-279):**
  - `avg10` = average of the last 10 trades (needs at least 3).
  - `last` = most recent trade.
  - `med3` and `med6` = 3-month and 6-month medians (need at least 3 trades).
- **State:** `S = {area: 84, homeArea: 59, homeId: 0, openId}`.

### Main functions
- `homePrice()` 282, `renderHome` 284, `renderList` 292 (sorted by |gap|), `chart` 319 (12-month SVG), `detail` 334, `refresh()` 484, `regen()` 485, init 486-494.

### Funding calculator (`calc()`, 362-376)
- **Inputs:** `p_sale, loan, fam, fin, mon (10,000 won/month), bonus (10,000 won/year), date (YYYY-MM), new, target, price, acq, cgt, etc`. Amounts are in 억 unless noted.
- **Formulas:**
  - months = (target year − this year) × 12 + (target month − 1 − this month)
  - Bonuses = floor(months / 12)
  - Savings = (mon × months + bonus × bonuses) / 10000
  - net = sale − loan − fam − cgt
  - equity = net + fin + savings
  - total = equity + new
  - need = price + acq + etc
  - lack = need − total. If positive, the page shows how much more equity is needed; otherwise it shows that funds are enough.
- **`autoAcq()` (359), acquisition tax:**
  - Rate: 1% up to 6억; p × 2/3 − 3 (%) from 6억 to 9억; 3% above 9억.
  - The result is multiplied by 1.1 to include education tax.
  - Not covered: the rural special tax (농특세), multi-home surcharges, and first-home buyer reductions.
- **Not modeled:** price growth, interest, LTV/DSR limits. Capital gains tax is entered by hand.

### 3D view (379-470)
- Number of buildings = clamp(households/190, 4, 14).
- Building height depends on construction year: before 2000 = 13 floors, before 2010 = 22, before 2018 = 28, otherwise 34. Each building is scaled ×0.75–1.25.
- The layout is deterministic per complex (`lcg(id*977+13)`).
- Two modes:
  - Orbit: distance 40–420.
  - Walk: eye height 1.7, WASD or the on-screen pad.
- Esc closes the view.

### Gotchas / bugs
1. On desktop, `.row` is a 6-column grid but has 7 children (50), so the ↗ column wraps. Mobile is fine.
2. Changing the unit size does not reset `P.price`/`P.sale`, because `syncPrice` only fills values that are null.
3. `regen()` erases prices the user entered.
4. If your home has fewer than 3 trades, every gap shows "—" and the sale price becomes 0.
5. In browsers that don't support `type=month`, the result becomes NaN.
6. The 3D view never disposes its geometry or textures. Lines 432 and 371 contain no-op code.
7. The guide card (224) says education tax is separate, but the code includes it with ×1.1.
